# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The short version is below. The full text at that link applies where this page is silent.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part without harassment, whatever their age, background, experience, language, disability, gender, ethnicity, religion or nationality. Modding communities are friendly when people are patient with each other, so let's keep it that way.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to modding or to GitHub.
- Give credit. Name the original mod authors and link to their work.
- Disagree about the code or the idea, never about the person.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it constructively.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Posting someone's private information.
- Sharing or asking for pirated, stolen, or re-uploaded mods in this project's issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Spam and unrelated promotion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use the contact details on [fawazbhseo.in](https://fawazbhseo.in) or open a private report through the repository's Security tab. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential.
